ClickFix Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

ClickFix connects customers with service workers and hardware shops. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email, phone number, password (stored hashed), profile photo.
  • Location: addresses you save, and for workers, live location while travelling to an active job (including in the background) so the customer can track arrival. Location sharing stops when the job ends.
  • Worker documents: ID, experience and education documents uploaded for verification.
  • Job and payment information: bookings, prices, payment method and status. Card payments are processed by PayHere; we do not store card numbers.
  • Messages and reviews: chat messages between customers and workers, ratings and reviews.
  • Hardware shop details: shop name, location, inventory and orders.

How we use it

  • To create and run your account and match customers with workers.
  • To show job progress, live arrival tracking, and route maps.
  • To verify workers' identity and qualifications.
  • To process payments, send notifications, and handle complaints.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • The other party in a job (e.g. a customer sees the assigned worker's name, photo and live location).
  • Service providers that run the app: MongoDB Atlas (database), Cloudinary (file storage), Google Maps (maps and geocoding), Firebase (authentication), PayHere (payments).
  • Authorities, where required by law.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete account in the app, or by following the steps at /delete-account. Deleting removes your account profile and notifications. Records of completed jobs and payments may be kept as long as required for accounting and legal reasons, without being linked to an active account.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and passwords are hashed.

Children

ClickFix is not intended for children under 13.
