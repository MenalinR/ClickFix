Delete your ClickFix account

In the app

  1. Open ClickFix and log in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

If you have an active job, finish or cancel it first.

Without the app

Email menalin4@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my ClickFix account". We will delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your profile (name, email, phone, photo, saved location, uploaded documents) and notifications. Records of completed jobs and payments may be kept as required for accounting and legal reasons. See our Privacy Policy.
